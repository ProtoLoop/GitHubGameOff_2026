Help

Select a material to edit its layer. Left-click or drag to fill cells and clear higher layers at those cells. Select the base to reveal the background. Right-click clears only the selected overlay.

Use the wheel to scroll. Hold Alt (Option on Mac) and use the wheel to zoom around the cursor. Drag with the middle mouse button to pan.

Edits autosave to the project's level.json. Ctrl/Cmd+S saves now. Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes and Shift+Ctrl/Cmd+Z redoes. Reload Project picks up asset changes.

Layers composite from bottom to top. Each square tile sheet must divide into an even grid at the shared tile size. Atlas tiles and junction masks are chosen automatically when painting.
